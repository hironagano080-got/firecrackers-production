# Saturday's Mine 歌詞

```
[Verse 1]
Friday night, I'm out the door
Got six good plans and room for more
A basement show, a midnight drive
God, it's good to be alive!

I crank the speakers way too loud
Sing every word and get 'em wrong
No one says, "Not that song again!"
So I let it play all night long

[Pre-Chorus 1]
One more night, one more street
I've got more time than I could ever need!

[Chorus 1]
Saturday's mine, Sunday's too!
Got a hundred stupid things to do
Late nights, loud tunes, sleep till ten
Play that stupid song again

Green lights, good times, nothing to lose
Nobody cares what I choose
I can take my sweet time getting home
No one's blowing up my phone

[Verse 2]
Saturday, I sleep till two
Eat cold pizza in my room
I take the good side of the bed
And leave the bathroom light on

Bought two tickets for the show
Told the guy one's for my coat
My friends keep asking how I've been
"The best!" I say with a stupid grin

[Pre-Chorus 2]
One more night, one more street
I've got more time than I could ever need!

[Chorus 2]
（Chorus 1と同じ）

[Bridge — short, instruments drop, solo vocal]
Ha, I'm such a loser
I'd give anything to hear you say
"Not that song again."

[Final Chorus — full band, gang vocals, louder]
(SATURDAY'S MINE!) Sunday's too!
Got a hundred stupid things to do
Late nights, loud tunes, sleep till ten
(PLAY THAT STUPID SONG AGAIN!)

(GREEN LIGHTS!) Good times, nothing to lose
Nobody cares what I choose
I can take my sweet time getting home
(NO ONE'S BLOWING UP MY PHONE!)

[End]
```

## 読み
別れた後の「自由」を強がって歌い、Bridgeで本音が漏れる。V1の「Not that song again! と誰も言わない」がBridgeで回収され、V2の「チケット2枚、1枚はコート用」が孤独の伏線。ラスサビの叫びは「失恋した仲間を、バンドがバカ騒ぎで慰める」絵（Hiroの読み）。
